Experiment design

koi-grill-experiment

A new card or experiment specification

Turn an experiment idea into a clear plan, one question at a time, before any run.

What it does

This is planning, not execution. The AI assistant interviews you with one question and a recommended answer per turn. It covers the claim, metric and verdict rule, boundaries, implementation, tables and charts, subtasks, and definition of done. The result is a draft specification in the report and a backlog card.

Why use it

Prevent experiments from starting with a vague hypothesis or ambiguous success criterion.

When to use it

  • You have a broad idea but need an experiment card
  • The specification lacks a metric, subtasks, or a verdict threshold
  • You need to record decisions one question at a time

How to invoke it

Ask the AI assistant to design or grill an experiment for a specific method, one question per turn. Use the separate card-execution workflow for the run itself.

Example request for the AI assistant in your editor:

Design a new experiment for method <method>:
grill the specification one question at a time and recommend an answer at every step.

Workflow diagram

Topics follow their dependencies: context and claim, metrics and boundaries, implementation and visualizations, then subtasks and a backlog card. A branch is skipped only when you explicitly say it is unnecessary.

flowchart TD
  A[Project context] --> B[Claim and motivation]
  B --> C[Metric and verdict rule]
  C --> D[Boundaries and dependencies]
  D --> E[Data, model, launch command]
  E --> F[Tables and charts]
  F --> G[Subtasks and definition of done]
  G --> H[Backlog card]
  H --> I[Draft report specification]
