Finding from a completed card

koi-done-research

A card reaches the done column

Turn a completed experiment into a plain-language question and answer stored in the findings database.

What it does

Reads the method context, card, and report, then writes a research question and answer understandable without project jargon. Technical figures are separated. It labels the finding as definite or tentative, records its importance, and saves it in the project's answer database.

Why use it

Leave a concise, reusable answer after every experiment, not just a report.

When to use it

  • A completed card has no structured finding
  • A result must be explained without internal jargon
  • The knowledge base needs certainty and importance metadata

How to invoke it

Ask the assistant to process a completed card and save its research finding. Provide the project and card ID or title.

Example request for the AI assistant in your editor:

Process completed card <id> in project <project>:
formulate a clear research question and answer, then save the finding.

Workflow diagram

Gather the method, card, and report; formulate a clear question and answer; separate technical detail; assign certainty and importance; save the result.
