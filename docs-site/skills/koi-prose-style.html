Research prose style

koi-prose-style

Writing or polishing reports, documentation, and papers

Edit research text into clear prose without filler or invented claims.

What it does

Improves structure, clarity, terminology, and sentence rhythm while preserving technical meaning and evidence boundaries. It removes generic filler, repetition, fake quotations, and unsupported certainty.

Why use it

Make research writing readable and credible without changing what the experiment established.

When to use it

  • A correct report is hard to read
  • A public document contains repetitive or AI-like phrasing
  • Terminology and claims need consistency

How to invoke it

Ask the assistant to edit a document with this workflow while preserving facts, citations, uncertainty, and terminology.

Example request for the AI assistant in your editor:

Edit this report with the research-prose workflow:
make it clear and concise while preserving every fact, citation, and uncertainty boundary.

Workflow diagram

Identify audience and claims, check terminology and evidence boundaries, rewrite for clarity, remove filler, and verify meaning preservation.
